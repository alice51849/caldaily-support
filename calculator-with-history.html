Why a plain calculator falls short

Most calculators forget the moment you close them. You check a sale price, switch apps, come back, and the number is gone. Everyday maths such as discounts, tax, splitting a bill or comparing loans is awkward on a plain keypad.

  • No history: you cannot find last week's split or loan comparison.
  • Retyping: the same tax rate or party size, every single time.
  • Extra taps: opening an app just to add two numbers.

Step by step: calculate, name and find results in CalDaily

  1. Add the CalDaily widget to your Home Screen. It runs the calculator without opening the app and matches your theme.
  2. Open a tool such as discount and tax, and set your defaults once, like your usual tax rate.
  3. Enter the price and the discount to see the result, then add tax if you need it.
  4. Give the result a name and a type, for example "winter jacket sale".
  5. Later, open history: it groups itself by day, filters by tool and exports to CSV for a spreadsheet.

A naming template that keeps history searchable

  • Shopping: "[item] – [x]% off"
  • Dining: "[restaurant] – split [people]"
  • Loans: "[lender] – [term]"
  • Work: "[project] – deadline"

Using the same pattern every time makes filtering and searching history quick, and the CSV export stays tidy if you move the numbers into a spreadsheet.
