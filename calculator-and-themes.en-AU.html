Searching for a calculator and themes usually means you want two things: a calculator that looks the way you like, and one that is quick to reach. What the themes change

One hundred themes are built into the app, from quiet ceramics to deep prisms. Each one has its own key shape, material and light, so switching theme changes the whole keypad, not just a colour.

Set up the widget

  1. Open CalDaily and choose a theme you like.
  2. Add the CalDaily widget to your Home Screen.
  3. Calculate straight from the widget without opening the app.

Name and find results

Name any result and search for it later instead of working it out again. History groups itself by day, filters by tool and exports to CSV for your spreadsheet. More on this in calculator with history.

Free theme and single purchase

One theme is free. A single purchase unlocks the rest along with unlimited use of every advanced tool. There is no subscription. Everything happens on your device: no account, no cloud sync, no advertising and no analytics. The app is fully localised in 50 languages.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to open the app to calculate?

No. The Home Screen widget calculates without opening the app.

How many themes are there?

One hundred themes are built into the app, each with its own key shape, material and light. One is free.

What does the single purchase unlock?

A single purchase unlocks the rest of the themes along with unlimited use of every advanced tool. There is no subscription.

Can I work out GST and discounts?

Yes. Discount and tax is one of the eight advanced tools, and each tool opens with the defaults you set once, such as your usual tax rate.

Does it need an account or show ads?

No account, no cloud sync, no advertising and no analytics. Everything happens on your device.

GST, discount and loan results in CalDaily depend on the numbers you enter. Check them against the actual receipt or agreement before making a financial decision.
