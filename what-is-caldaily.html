What is CalDaily?

CalDaily is an iPhone calculator that remembers. You can name any result and search for it later, instead of working it out again. It has 100 built-in themes, a Home Screen widget that calculates without opening the app, and eight tools: tip and split, discount and tax, loan payments, unit conversion, date differences and business days, trip fuel cost, memory and scientific functions. History groups itself by day, filters by tool and exports to CSV.

Who is CalDaily for?

Anyone who splits bills, checks discounts, converts units for recipes or DIY, plans dates and deadlines, compares loan options, or simply wants a calculator that looks good on the Home Screen. Each tool opens with the defaults you set once, such as a tax rate or party size.

How does CalDaily charge?

One theme is included, and a single purchase unlocks the rest along with unlimited use of every advanced tool. The listing says there is no subscription, no account, no cloud sync, no advertising and no analytics. For the current price, check the App Store page, because prices differ by country.

How is CalDaily different from the built-in calculator?

Based on what the listing describes: results can be named and searched later; history is grouped by day, filterable and exportable to CSV; there are eight task-specific tools with saved defaults; the Home Screen widget calculates without opening the app; and each of the 100 themes has its own key shape, material and light. Everything stays on your device, and the app is localized in 50 languages.
